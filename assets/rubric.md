# Capstone Evaluation Rubric

Used to grade the Week 12 capstone project. Total: 100 points.

## Technical Soundness — 40 pts

| Criterion | Excellent (10) | Proficient (7) | Basic (4) |
|-----------|----------------|----------------|-----------|
| **Problem framing** | Crisp scope, well-motivated, novel angle | Clear scope, standard motivation | Vague or over-broad |
| **Method selection** | Justified with ablation or citation | Reasonable, partially justified | Default choices, unjustified |
| **Implementation quality** | Reproducible, tested, well-structured | Reproducible, minor issues | Hard to reproduce |
| **Evaluation rigor** | Multiple metrics, baselines, CIs | Multiple metrics, baselines | Single metric, no baseline |

## Scientific Communication — 30 pts

| Criterion | Excellent (10) | Proficient (7) | Basic (4) |
|-----------|----------------|----------------|-----------|
| **Report writing** | NeurIPS-quality prose and figures | Clear, well-organized | Functional but rough |
| **Failure analysis** | Honest, specific, instructive | Honest, somewhat specific | Surface-level |
| **Visualizations** | Custom, informative, labeled | Standard plots, clear | Default plots only |

## Originality and Insight — 20 pts

| Criterion | Excellent (10) | Proficient (7) | Basic (4) |
|-----------|----------------|----------------|-----------|
| **Insight beyond execution** | Genuine finding worth sharing | One non-obvious observation | Reports results only |
| **Connection to prior work** | Cites and engages with literature | Cites literature | Minimal engagement |

## Presentation — 10 pts

| Criterion | Excellent (10) | Proficient (7) | Basic (4) |
|-----------|----------------|----------------|-----------|
| **15-minute talk + Q&A** | Compelling story, fields questions well | Clear talk, answers most questions | Reads slides, struggles in Q&A |
